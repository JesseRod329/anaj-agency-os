# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in communication and review feedback.
- Assume positive intent.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Share context and rationale when disagreeing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech
- Personal attacks or intimidation
- Repeated disruptive behavior after feedback
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards.

Reports can be sent privately to: **maintainers@anaj.dev**.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
